# Sarach portfolio site — glossary

The personal site of Sarach (Aou), the person behind NorthBear. It builds
trust in Aou and points visitors to NorthBear to buy.

## Terms

**Site owner** — Sarach (Aou). The site is about this person, not about
NorthBear as a company.

**NorthBear** — Aou's data/AI education and consulting brand. The site points
to it; it is not the site itself.

**Primary visitor** — a prospective Client or Student. The site is written
for them first.

**Client** — a business (usually an SME) that might hire Aou/NorthBear for
done-for-you dashboards or pipelines.

**Student** — someone who might buy a NorthBear course.

**Recruiter** — a secondary visitor, usually arriving from the CV link. Served
by the same proof of work; never the main audience. The site does not
advertise job-seeking.

**Core idea** — "A BI analyst who works alongside AI, shown for real"
(TH: "BI Analyst ที่ทำงานคู่กับ AI ให้ดูกันจริง ๆ"). Every page serves this
line. Shown, not claimed: where possible a visitor can try the thing.

**Core BI skill** — the tool-independent craft the site leads with: turning a
business question into a metric, modelling the data, telling the story, and
driving a decision. Tools (dbt, Redshift, Evidence, Power BI…) are named only
as evidence, never as the identity. Avoid: "Power BI expert".

**Human / AI split** — for every Work item, the site says what Aou did and
what AI did. This split is the site's signature; a Work item without it is
incomplete.

**Work** — anything shown as proof of skill. Exactly one of three kinds,
always labelled:
- **Day-job work** — done for an employer (Juzmatch, Minor, Predictive).
  Shown as a story only: no real data, screenshots, or internal names beyond
  what a CV would say.
- **Personal work** — Aou's own builds (e.g. the stock pipeline). Can be
  shown in full, with code and live demos.
- **NorthBear work** — NorthBear products (e.g. Echo, the Blueprint). Shown
  as proof and as the route to buying.

**Flagship** — the single Work item shown first everywhere. Currently Echo,
the AI data agent from the Blueprint.

**Look** — one theme for both languages, in NorthBear colours (cream page,
navy text, gold accents; gold as *text* uses the darker #7A5A12 so it stays
readable). Fonts: Kanit for headings, IBM Plex Sans Thai for body, Prompt for
menus and buttons. Avoid: a separate Thai theme.

**Data story** — the homepage: one hotel owner's question taken to one
decision in six steps, on the Phra Nang House sample data, with the Human /
AI split at every step. It is the site's proof of the Core idea.

**Phra Nang House** — the fictional 30-room hotel whose sample data powers the
data story, the Playground and the NorthBear courses. Always called fictional;
never presented as a client.

**Playground** — the page of things to try (Echo demo, games, the data health
check). Things to try, not things to read.

**North Star** — one of six collectibles hidden across the site, each earned
by doing something (finishing the story, asking Echo, finishing a game).
Kept in the visitor's own browser only.

**Ladder** — the order the site sells in: try free → NorthBear Blueprint →
done-for-you work.

**Language** — every visible text exists in both Thai (TH) and English (EN);
the visitor switches between them. A page is not done until both exist.
