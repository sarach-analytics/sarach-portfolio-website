Echo, an AI data agent for your spreadsheet

A dashboard answers the first question. Echo answers the next five.

Echo turns a business spreadsheet into a morning recap: what changed, why, and what to do next. Then you can ask it follow-up questions in plain language. It's the AI data agent you build in the NorthBear Blueprint course.

What I did
Chose the metrics and the questions Echo must answer, designed the recap dashboard, and set the rules: show the source of every number, and say "I don't know" instead of guessing.
What AI does
Reads the sheet, explains period-over-period changes, writes the narrative brief, and answers follow-up questions, with a link back to the exact rows it used.

  • An honest date badge: you always know how fresh the data is

How I work

Core BI skills, with AI as a co-pilot

Tools change: I've shipped with Power BI, dbt, Redshift and Evidence. The craft doesn't. These four steps are the same on every project, and so is the split between me and AI.

01

Question → metric

Agree what the business is really asking, and define one metric everyone trusts.

Me
Interview stakeholders, settle the definition, own the single source of truth.
AI
Drafts metric definitions and the data dictionary for me to review.
02

Model the data

Build one central model so the logic lives in one place, not inside every report.

Me
Design the model, the grain and the tests; review every line.
AI
Writes first-draft SQL and data tests, and spots inconsistencies.
03

Tell the story

Show what changed and why, and leave out everything that doesn't help the decision.

Me
Choose what matters, design the dashboard, decide what to cut.
AI
Explains variance and drafts the written summary.
04

Drive the decision

Insight only counts when someone acts on it, so I make sure people can use the answer themselves.

Me
Make the recommendation, train users, follow through.
AI
Answers follow-up questions self-serve, like Echo.

AI writes the first draft. I own the answer.
