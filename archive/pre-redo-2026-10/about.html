About

I'm Sarach (Aou). I turn business questions into decisions, with AI as my co-pilot.

I trained as an industrial engineer, then found my way into data: first digital analytics, then BI for a 500+ hotel group, and now analytics engineering at Juzmatch. Along the way the tools kept changing. What stayed the same is the craft: agree the question, define the metric, model the data, tell the story, and make sure someone acts on it.

Outside work I run NorthBear, named after the Great Bear, the stars that point to the North Star. NorthBear helps analysts and small businesses find the one metric that matters, through courses and done-for-you dashboards and pipelines.
